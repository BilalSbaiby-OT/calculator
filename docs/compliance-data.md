## Content Standards

### How to Write Each Item

**Title:** Short, imperative. Max 60 characters. E.g., "Display registration number on listing" not "You must display your Airbnb registration number on your listing at all times."

**Short description:** One sentence. What it is. Max 120 characters.

**How to comply:** Numbered steps. Link to the official page for each step. No jargon. Write it as if explaining to someone who has never done this before.

**Penalty:** Always state as a range with the specific regulation cited. Don't write "you could be fined" — write "Fine: €450 per offense (Article L324-1-1 Code du Tourisme)."

**Difficulty:** 1 = fill out one online form. 2 = requires gathering documents or in-person visit. 3 = complex multi-step process, may require professional help.

## PARIS, FRANCE

**Official source:** paris.fr/pages/location-meublée-de-tourisme-5064
**Country code:** FR  
**Language:** French  
**Key law:** Code du Tourisme, Article L324-1-1; Règlement Métropolitain

---

### Item P-01: Municipal Declaration (Déclaration en mairie)
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Complete municipal declaration (déclaration en mairie)
- **Short description:** All short-term rental hosts in Paris must declare their property to the city.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** One-time (on commencement of rental activity)
- **Difficulty:** 1
- **Penalty:** Fine up to €450 per offense
- **Official URL:** https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2043
- **How to comply:**
  1. Visit service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2043
  2. Complete the online declaration form (Cerfa n°14004)
  3. Receive your registration number (numéro de déclaration) — 13 digits
  4. Store a copy of your confirmation email
- **Notes:** This applies only if you rent an entire furnished dwelling. If you rent a room in your primary residence, declaration is not required but the 120-night limit still applies.

---

### Item P-02: Display Registration Number on Listing
- **Category:** display_requirements
- **Title:** Add registration number to all listing platforms
- **Short description:** Your 13-digit registration number must appear on every listing and rental ad.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** Event-based (must be done before first rental)
- **Difficulty:** 1
- **Penalty:** Fine up to €12,500
- **Official URL:** https://www.paris.fr/pages/location-meublée-de-tourisme-5064
- **How to comply:**
  1. On Airbnb: Go to Your Listings → Edit → Policies and rules → Add registration number
  2. On Booking.com: Property → Policies → Enter license/registration number
  3. Verify it appears on your public listing page
- **Notes:** Airbnb automatically requests this number from Paris hosts. Check that the number displayed matches exactly your déclaration confirmation.

---

### Item P-03: Respect the 120-Night Annual Limit
- **Category:** operational_limits
- **Title:** Track and respect the 120-night annual rental limit
- **Short description:** You can only rent your primary residence as an STR for 120 nights per calendar year.
- **Mandatory:** Yes (for primary residence only)
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house, private_room
- **Deadline type:** Annual (resets January 1)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Fine up to €50,000 + obligation to reconvert to residential use
- **Official URL:** https://www.paris.fr/pages/location-meublée-de-tourisme-5064
- **How to comply:**
  1. Track your rented nights manually or via Airbnb's "nights booked" counter
  2. Stop accepting bookings once you reach 120 nights for the calendar year
  3. Airbnb automatically blocks further bookings in Paris once 120 nights are reached — verify this is activated in your listing settings
- **Notes:** This limit applies ONLY to your primary residence. If this is a secondary property (not where you live), you need a change of use permit (changement d'usage) — this is a separate, complex process requiring a Property type = entire_apartment or entire_house.

---

### Item P-04: Change of Use Permit (Secondary Properties Only)
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Obtain change of use permit (changement d'usage)
- **Short description:** Required if the property is NOT your primary residence and you rent it as a furnished tourist accommodation.
- **Mandatory:** Yes (for non-primary residence only)
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** One-time
- **Difficulty:** 3
- **Penalty:** Fine up to €50,000 + forced reconversion to residential use
- **Official URL:** https://www.paris.fr/pages/louer-son-logement-en-meublé-de-tourisme-5064
- **How to comply:**
  1. Submit application to Direction du Logement et de l'Habitat (DLH) Paris
  2. Requires compensatory measure: provide equivalent residential floor space elsewhere in Paris
  3. This is complex — highly recommend professional assistance
  4. Processing time: 3–6 months
- **Notes:** This item applies to ~20% of hosts. It is the most complex compliance item in Paris. Many hosts are unknowingly non-compliant. If you are unsure whether your property is your primary residence in the legal sense, consult a notaire or avocat.

---

### Item P-05: Tourist Tax Registration (Taxe de Séjour)
- **Category:** tax_registration
- **Title:** Register for and collect tourist tax (taxe de séjour)
- **Short description:** Paris hosts must collect a per-person-per-night tourist tax from guests.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** Quarterly (due 15th of month after quarter end)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Tax assessment + 40% penalty for non-declaration
- **Official URL:** https://www.paris.fr/pages/la-taxe-de-sejour-5065
- **How to comply:**
  1. Airbnb and Booking.com collect and remit taxe de séjour automatically in Paris — verify this is enabled in your host settings
  2. If using other platforms, register at taxedesejour.paris.fr
  3. If self-collecting: declare and pay quarterly via the platform
- **Notes:** Most hosts using Airbnb do NOT need to manually collect this — Airbnb handles it. But if you also host directly (your own website, direct bookings), you must handle it yourself for those bookings.

---

### Item P-06: Primary Residence Proof
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Maintain proof that this is your primary residence
- **Short description:** You must be able to prove this is your primary residence if audited by the city.
- **Mandatory:** Yes (for primary residence listings)
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house, private_room
- **Deadline type:** One-time (keep on file)
- **Difficulty:** 1
- **Penalty:** Reclassification as tourist accommodation requiring changement d'usage
- **Official URL:** https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2040
- **How to comply:**
  1. Keep a copy of: (a) tax notice showing this address, (b) utility bill in your name, (c) lease or property deed
  2. Upload these documents to HostCompliant's document storage
  3. French law defines primary residence as where you live >8 months per year

## BARCELONA, SPAIN

**Official source:** ajuntament.barcelona.cat/turisme/ca/habitatge-us-turistic
**Country code:** ES  
**Language:** Spanish/Catalan  
**Key law:** Decree 159/2012 on tourist establishments; Plan Especial Urbanístic (PEU)

**⚠️ Critical context:** Barcelona's city council voted in November 2023 to NOT renew any existing HUTB licenses when they expire in November 2028. Barcelona is the hardest STR market in Europe. New HUTB licenses are essentially impossible to obtain since 2014. Existing license holders must comply fully and renew annually until 2028.

---

### Item B-01: Hold a Valid HUTB License
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Maintain valid HUTB tourist housing license
- **Short description:** A HUTB (Habitatge d'Ús Turístic de Barcelona) license is required to legally rent short-term in Barcelona.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** Annual (renewal by November 30 each year)
- **Difficulty:** 3
- **Penalty:** Fine €3,001–€90,000
- **Official URL:** https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/turisme/ca/habitatge-us-turistic
- **How to comply:**
  1. If you already have a HUTB: renew annually before November 30 via the Generalitat de Catalunya portal
  2. If you do NOT have a HUTB: you cannot legally operate an entire-apartment STR in Barcelona. New licenses are not being issued.
  3. Renewal requires: current license, proof of habitation, no outstanding complaints
- **Notes:** MOST IMPORTANT item for Barcelona hosts. Without a valid HUTB, all STR activity is illegal and carries fines up to €90,000. Check your license expiry date immediately.

---

### Item B-02: Display HUTB License Number on Listing
- **Category:** display_requirements
- **Title:** Display HUTB license number on every listing
- **Short description:** Your HUTB number must appear on Airbnb, Booking.com, and any other platform listing.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** Event-based (ongoing)
- **Difficulty:** 1
- **Penalty:** Part of the €90,000 maximum penalty regime
- **Official URL:** https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/turisme/ca/habitatge-us-turistic
- **How to comply:**
  1. On Airbnb: Listing → Policies and rules → Local laws and taxes → Add license number
  2. On Booking.com: Property → Registration number field
  3. Format: HUT/XXXXXXXXX/XXXX

---

### Item B-03: Register Guests with Police (via GES)
- **Category:** guest_reporting
- **Title:** Register all guests with Mossos d'Esquadra within 24 hours of check-in
- **Short description:** Spanish law requires tourist accommodation to register each guest's passport/ID with police within 24 hours of arrival.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** Event-based (per guest check-in)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Serious infraction: €30,000–€600,000
- **Official URL:** https://web.gencat.cat/ca/tramits/tramits-temes/Comunicacio-allotjament
- **How to comply:**
  1. Register at the Mossos d'Esquadra online platform (GES — Gestió d'Establiments)
  2. For each guest check-in: enter ID number, nationality, name, dates within 24 hours
  3. Guest must be 14+ to register; for minors, register parent/guardian
  4. Keep records for 3 years
- **Notes:** This is often overlooked. Airbnb does NOT do this for you. You must do it for every guest. Non-compliance is a serious infraction. There are paid tools (Chekin, Hospitable) that automate this — worth the investment.

---

### Item B-04: Collect and Remit Tourist Tax (Taxa Turística)
- **Category:** tax_registration
- **Title:** Collect and remit Catalan tourist tax
- **Short description:** The Generalitat de Catalunya imposes a per-person-per-night tourist tax for tourist accommodation.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** Quarterly (due last day of month following quarter end)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Tax assessment + surcharges
- **Official URL:** https://atc.gencat.cat/ca/tributs/taxes/impost-sobre-estades-en-establiments-turistics
- **How to comply:**
  1. Airbnb collects and remits this tax automatically — verify in Host settings → Taxes
  2. For other platforms: register at ATC (Agència Tributària de Catalunya) and file quarterly
  3. Rate (2026): €1.50–€4.00 per person per night (varies by accommodation type)

---

### Item B-05: Obtain Habitability Certificate
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Hold a valid habitability certificate (cèdula d'habitabilitat)
- **Short description:** Required to obtain and renew a HUTB license.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** One-time (valid 15 years for existing buildings, 25 years for new)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Cannot obtain/renew HUTB without it
- **Official URL:** https://web.gencat.cat/ca/tramits/tramits-temes/Cedula-dhabitabilitat
- **How to comply:**
  1. Contact a qualified architect (arquitecte tècnic) to inspect the property
  2. Architect issues a report; submit to Departament de Territori
  3. Processing: 1–3 months; cost: €100–€300 architect fee + €50 government fee

## AMSTERDAM, NETHERLANDS

**Official source:** amsterdam.nl/wonen-leefomgeving/wonen/vakantieverhuur
**Country code:** NL  
**Language:** Dutch  
**Key law:** Huisvestingswet, Tijdelijk verhuur regels Amsterdam

---

### Item A-01: Register as Short-term Rental Host
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Register your property as a short-term rental
- **Short description:** Amsterdam requires all STR hosts to register with the municipality before renting.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** One-time (renewable annually)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Fine up to €22,500
- **Official URL:** https://www.amsterdam.nl/wonen-leefomgeving/wonen/vakantieverhuur/registratienummer-aanvragen/
- **How to comply:**
  1. Log in to amsterdam.nl with DigiD (Dutch digital ID)
  2. Apply for a registration number via the online form
  3. Receive registration number (starts with 0363)
  4. Add the number to your listing

---

### Item A-02: Obtain Short-stay Permit (for >30 nights)
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Apply for short-stay permit if renting more than 30 nights per year
- **Short description:** After 30 nights per year, you need a special permit to continue renting in Amsterdam.
- **Mandatory:** Yes (if renting >30 nights/year)
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** Annual
- **Difficulty:** 3
- **Penalty:** Fine up to €22,500 + forced cessation
- **Official URL:** https://www.amsterdam.nl/wonen-leefomgeving/wonen/vakantieverhuur/vergunning/
- **How to comply:**
  1. Apply via the municipality for a "shortstay" permit
  2. Application requires: property deed or lease, building plan, floor plan, proof of primary residence
  3. Processing time: 8–16 weeks
  4. Note: Permits are not automatically granted — availability depends on neighbourhood quota
- **Notes:** Amsterdam limits STR to 30 nights per year WITHOUT a permit. If you need more, the permit process is complex and limited. This item is often the reason hosts in Amsterdam cannot scale.

---

### Item A-03: Respect the 30-Night Annual Limit
- **Category:** operational_limits
- **Title:** Do not exceed 30 nights of STR without a permit
- **Short description:** Amsterdam caps short-term rentals at 30 nights per calendar year without a special permit.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** Annual (resets January 1)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Fine up to €22,500 per violation
- **Official URL:** https://www.amsterdam.nl/wonen-leefomgeving/wonen/vakantieverhuur/regels/
- **How to comply:**
  1. Track rented nights on a spreadsheet or via Airbnb's booking history
  2. Stop accepting bookings once you reach 30 nights for the calendar year (or apply for a permit)
  3. Amsterdam inspectors actively monitor via Airbnb data sharing agreements
- **Notes:** Amsterdam is the strictest city for the 30-night cap. Fines are actively enforced. Airbnb does NOT automatically block after 30 nights — you must manage this yourself.

---

### Item A-04: Display Registration Number on Listing
- **Category:** display_requirements
- **Title:** Display Amsterdam registration number on all listings
- **Short description:** Your municipal registration number must appear on every platform listing.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All

  1. Add to Airbnb: Listing → Local laws and taxes → Registration number (format: 0363XXXXXXXXX)
  2. Add to Booking.com: Property settings → Registration

---

### Item A-05: Pay Tourist Tax (Toeristenbelasting)
- **Category:** tax_registration
- **Title:** Register and pay Amsterdam tourist tax
- **Short description:** Amsterdam levies a per-person-per-night tourist tax.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** Quarterly
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Tax assessment + 100% surcharge for late payment
- **Official URL:** https://www.amsterdam.nl/parkeren-verkeer/parkeren/parkeertarieven/toeristenbelasting/
- **How to comply:**
  1. Airbnb collects toeristenbelasting automatically in Amsterdam — confirm in Host settings
  2. If collecting directly: register at amsterdam.nl and file quarterly returns
  3. Rate (2026): 7% of rental price + €3 per person per night

## LISBON, PORTUGAL

**Official source:** cm-lisboa.pt/viver/habitacao/alojamento-local
**Country code:** PT  
**Language:** Portuguese  
**Key law:** Decreto-Lei n.º 128/2014; Lei n.º 62/2018

---

### Item L-01: Alojamento Local Registration
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Register as Alojamento Local with the municipality
- **Short description:** All STR properties in Portugal require Alojamento Local (AL) registration.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** One-time (annual fee to maintain)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Fine €4,000–€40,000 for unregistered activity
- **Official URL:** https://balcaovirtual.cm-lisboa.pt/
- **How to comply:**
  1. Submit application via Balcão Único Eletrónico (BUE): balcaovirtual.cm-lisboa.pt
  2. Required documents: property deed or lease, ID, property floor plan
  3. The municipality must respond within 20 days (silence = approval)
  4. Receive your RNAL registration number
  5. Annual registration fee: €300–€700 depending on property category

---

### Item L-02: Display RNAL Number on Listings
- **Category:** display_requirements
- **Title:** Display RNAL number on all listings and at the property entrance
- **Short description:** The Registo Nacional de Alojamento Local number must appear on listings and on a plaque at the entrance.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** Event-based (ongoing)
- **Difficulty:** 1
- **Penalty:** Part of the AL registration violation fine regime
- **Official URL:** https://rnt.turismodeportugal.pt/
- **How to comply:**
  1. Add RNAL number to Airbnb and Booking.com listings
  2. Install the official AL identification plaque at property entrance (can be ordered from certified suppliers)
  3. Plaque format: Blue background with "AL" text + registration number

---

### Item L-03: Municipal Authorization (Lisbon Specific Zones)
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Obtain Lisbon municipal authorization (historic zones)
- **Short description:** Since 2019, new AL registrations in Lisbon's historic parishes require specific municipal authorization.
- **Mandatory:** Yes (if in Misericórdia, Santa Maria Maior, Mouraria, or Alfama zones)
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** One-time
- **Difficulty:** 3
- **Penalty:** Registration refused; operation illegal
- **Official URL:** https://www.cm-lisboa.pt/viver/habitacao/alojamento-local
- **How to comply:**
  1. Check if your property is in a "zona de contenção" (containment zone) using the CML map
  2. If yes: no new registrations are permitted
  3. Existing registrations in these zones: transferable when property is sold; suspendable when host moves out
- **Notes:** This affects many of the most popular tourist areas in Lisbon. If buying a property in Lisbon specifically to rent, check this first.

---

### Item L-04: Tourist Tax (Taxa Municipal Turística)
- **Category:** tax_registration
- **Title:** Collect and remit Lisbon tourist tax
- **Short description:** Lisbon charges a nightly tourist tax per adult guest.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** Quarterly
- **Difficulty:** 1
- **Penalty:** Tax assessment + 25% surcharge
- **Official URL:** https://www.cm-lisboa.pt/servicos/detalhe/taxa-municipal-turistica

  2. Rate (2026): €2.00 per adult per night, maximum 7 nights, April–October peak season €2.50

## ROME, ITALY

**Official source:** romaturismo.it; mimit.gov.it/it/turismo/strutture-ricettive
**Country code:** IT  
**Language:** Italian  
**Key law:** Decreto Legislativo 79/2011; Decreto del Ministero del Turismo August 2023 (CIN)

---

### Item R-01: Obtain National CIN Code (CRITICAL — 2024 Law)
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Register for national CIN code (Codice Identificativo Nazionale)
- **Short description:** Since September 2024, all STR properties in Italy must have a CIN code or face fines up to €8,000.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** One-time (then display ongoing)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Fine €800–€8,000 per property without CIN
- **Official URL:** https://www.bdsr.mimit.gov.it/
- **How to comply:**
  1. Register at bdsr.mimit.gov.it (Base Dati delle Strutture Ricettive — national database)
  2. Submit property details: address, floor plan, capacity, type
  3. Attach: property deed or lease, fire safety declaration, habitability certificate
  4. Receive CIN code within 30 days
  5. Add CIN to ALL listing platforms immediately
- **Notes:** This is the most urgent compliance item for Italian hosts. The law is actively enforced since January 2025. Airbnb has been sending reminders to Italian hosts. Many hosts received fines in 2025 for delayed registration.

---

### Item R-02: Display CIN on Listings and at Property
- **Category:** display_requirements
- **Title:** Display CIN code on listings, ads, and at property entrance
- **Short description:** The CIN code must appear on all digital and physical rental advertising and at the property entrance.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** Event-based (ongoing)
- **Difficulty:** 1
- **Penalty:** Fine €500–€5,000
- **Official URL:** https://www.bdsr.mimit.gov.it/
- **How to comply:**
  1. Add CIN to Airbnb listing: Host settings → Local laws → Registration/license number
  2. Add to Booking.com: Property → Registration details
  3. Display at property: official CIN plate or equivalent signage at entrance

---

### Item R-03: Municipal SCIA (Notification of Activity)
- **Category:** permits
- **Title:** Submit SCIA (Segnalazione Certificata di Inizio Attività) to municipality
- **Short description:** Starting an STR activity in Rome requires submitting a SCIA to the Comune di Roma.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** One-time (before first rental)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Irregular activity fine + potential suspension
- **Official URL:** https://www.comune.roma.it/web/it/accesso-rapido-al-servizio.page
- **How to comply:**
  1. Submit via the Comune di Roma's online SUAP portal
  2. Required: property deed or lease, floor plan, fire safety document, ATECO code (55.20.51 for tourist apartments)
  3. No waiting period — activity can start immediately after submission
  4. Keep a copy of the SCIA receipt indefinitely

---

### Item R-04: Fire Safety Declaration
- **Category:** safety
- **Title:** File fire safety self-declaration (dichiarazione antincendio)
- **Short description:** Properties with >6 guest capacity require fire safety compliance and certification.
- **Mandatory:** Yes (for properties accommodating >6 guests)
- **Property types:** entire_apartment, entire_house
- **Deadline type:** One-time
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Cannot obtain CIN or SCIA without it; safety violations: heavy fines
- **Official URL:** https://www.vigilfuoco.it/
- **How to comply:**
  1. For <6 guests: self-certification of basic safety measures (fire extinguisher, smoke detector, emergency exit)
  2. For 6+ guests: inspection by fire brigade (Vigili del Fuoco) required
  3. Book inspection at local fire brigade office
- **Notes:** All properties must have smoke detectors and CO detectors (EU standard). Fire extinguisher must be inspected within 6 years.

---

### Item R-05: Tourist Tax (Tassa di Soggiorno Roma)
- **Category:** tax_registration
- **Title:** Register and remit Rome tourist tax
- **Short description:** Rome charges a per-person-per-night tax collected from guests.
- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Property types:** All
- **Deadline type:** Quarterly (due January 30, April 30, July 30, October 30)
- **Difficulty:** 2
- **Penalty:** Tax assessment + 30% penalty + interest
- **Official URL:** https://www.comune.roma.it/web/it/strutture-ricettive.page
- **How to comply:**
  1. Register at the Comune di Roma tourist tax portal
  2. Airbnb collects and remits automatically in Rome — verify in Host settings
  3. For direct bookings: collect from each guest and remit quarterly via the municipality portal
  4. Rate (2026): €3.50–€7.00 per person per night (up to 10 nights maximum), varies by star rating
