🇪🇸 Barcelona STR Compliance Guide

Strict 5 requirements

Barcelona has some of the strictest short-term rental rules in Europe. A HUTB license is required to legally rent an entire apartment. New licenses are no longer issued. Fines reach €90,000.

📅 Last verified: June 2026 · View official sources

⚡ Key facts in 60 seconds

  • You must have a HUTB license to rent an entire apartment short-term. No new licenses have been issued since 2014.
  • All existing HUTB licenses expire in November 2028 and will not be renewed.
  • You must register every guest's ID with the Mossos d'Esquadra police within 24 hours of check-in. Every. Single. Guest.
  • Tourist tax is collected quarterly. Airbnb handles it automatically — but direct bookings, you handle manually.
  • Your HUTB number must be displayed on every listing. Fines for non-display are part of the €90,000 regime.

Register guests with Mossos d'Esquadra (GES)
Required within 24 hours of every guest check-in · Spanish law Decreto 155/2012
OVERDUE Fine: €30K–€600K
›

How to comply

  1. Register at the Mossos d'Esquadra GES portal: ges.mossos.gencat.cat
  2. For each guest check-in: enter full name, ID/passport number, nationality, dates of stay
  3. Submit within 24 hours of arrival. Keep records for 3 years.
  4. Guests under 14 can be registered under a parent or guardian's entry.

Why this matters

Spanish law requires all tourist accommodation to report guest identity to police. Barcelona inspectors have intensified checks in 2026. Non-compliance is classified as a serious infraction with fines starting at €30,000.

💡 Tip: Tools like Chekin or Hospitable can automate this for every booking.
